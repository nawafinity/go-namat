# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Namat community is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and
professional environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

We expect participation in this project to be guided by respect, empathy,
clarity, and a shared interest in improving the software and its community.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and keep technical discussions constructive.
- Welcome questions, differing viewpoints, and people who are still learning.
- Give and receive feedback with care, focusing on ideas and outcomes rather
  than individuals.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwelcome
  behavior.
- Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the
  experience.
- Protect confidential or sensitive information encountered while
  contributing.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, threats, or incitement of violence.
- Discrimination, demeaning stereotypes, or exclusion based on personal
  characteristics or identity.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal insults, sustained hostility, or deliberately inflammatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Disrupting project spaces through spam, repeated bad-faith arguments, or
  other conduct that prevents productive participation.
- Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern or helps review one.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, release channels, and community events. It also applies
when someone officially represents Namat in public or private spaces.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers using a private
contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue.

When possible, include links, dates, context, and any relevant evidence. Good-
faith reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. A
maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves from reviewing it.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that
violate this code. Depending on the nature and history of the behavior,
responses may include clarification, a private warning, a temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be timely, consistent, proportionate, and
documented privately. The privacy and safety of reporters and affected people
take priority over public disclosure.
